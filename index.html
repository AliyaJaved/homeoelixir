FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to help you understand homeopathy and our personalized approach to wellness.

Can Homeopathy be taken with Allopathic or Ayurvedic medicines?

Yes. Homeopathic medicines can generally be taken along with Allopathic or Ayurvedic medicines. You do not need to stop your ongoing medicines unless your doctor advises you to.

What conditions can Homeopathy help manage?

Homeopathy is commonly used to support individuals with allergies, digestive concerns, hormonal imbalances, skin conditions, hair problems, migraines, stress-related disorders, respiratory issues, joint discomfort, and many other acute and chronic health concerns.

Does Homeopathy have any side effects?

Homeopathic products are often highly diluted, so side effects are generally uncommon. However, some products may contain active ingredients, so they should still be taken under proper guidance.

How long does Homeopathic treatment take?

It depends on the condition and the individual. Acute problems may improve sooner, while long-standing conditions may require longer treatment. Regular follow-ups help assess progress.
