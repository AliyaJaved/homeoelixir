Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about homeopathy, treatments, consultations, and your wellness journey.

1. What conditions can Homeopathy help manage?

Homeopathy is commonly used to support individuals with allergies, digestive concerns, hormonal imbalances, skin conditions, hair problems, migraines, stress-related disorders, respiratory issues, joint discomfort, and many other acute and chronic health concerns.

2. Does Homeopathy only treat symptoms?

Homeopathy takes an individualised approach, considering the person’s symptoms and overall health. It is not limited to looking at one symptom alone. However, treatment depends on the condition and proper diagnosis.

3. What are the sources of Homeopathic medicines?

Homeopathic preparations may originate from plant, mineral and animal sources, as well as some other substances, and are prepared according to Homeopathic methods.

4. Can Homeopathy help prevent recurring illnesses?

Homeopathy aims to improve the body's overall balance and resilience, which may help reduce the frequency of recurring health concerns for some individuals. Results vary from person to person.

5. Does Homeopathy have any side effects?

Homeopathic products are often highly diluted, so side effects are generally uncommon. However, some products may contain active ingredients, so they should still be taken under proper guidance.

6. Do Homeopathic medicines contain steroids?

No. Genuine Homeopathic medicines are not supposed to contain steroids. Always use medicines from a reliable source and under the guidance of a qualified Homeopathic doctor.

7. Are Homeopathic medicines addictive?

No. Homeopathic medicines are not considered habit-forming or addictive when used appropriately.

8. Can homeopathic pills cause my symptoms to temporarily increase?

Sometimes, patients experience a mild, temporary flare-up of their symptoms shortly after starting treatment. This is known as a homeopathic aggravation. It is generally a positive sign indicating that your body's healing mechanism has reacted to the remedy and is actively clearing out the illness. This phase passes quickly and is almost always followed by steady improvement.

9. Can homeopathy be taken with Allopathic or Ayurvedic medicines?

Yes. Homeopathic medicines can generally be taken along with Allopathic or Ayurvedic medicines. You do not need to stop your ongoing medicines unless your doctor advises you to.

10. How long does Homeopathic treatment take?

It depends on the condition and the individual. Acute problems may improve sooner, while long-standing conditions may require longer treatment. Regular follow-ups help assess progress.

11. How is a Homeopathic consultation different from a regular consultation?

A Homeopathic consultation usually involves detailed case-taking. Along with your physical symptoms, the doctor may ask about your sleep, appetite, lifestyle, emotional well-being, medical history and other individual factors before selecting treatment.

12. Can diabetic patients take Homeopathy even though the globules are sweet?

Yes. Diabetic patients can take Homeopathic medicines. The amount of sugar in the small globules is usually very low, but sugar-free or liquid forms can also be considered, especially when blood sugar needs strict control.

13. Can Homeopathy help change a person’s behaviour?

Homeopathy cannot directly change someone’s personality or behaviour. Behavioural or emotional concerns may improve when their underlying causes are properly addressed, often with counselling, psychological care, or medical treatment when needed.

14. Can Homeopathy help in overcoming addictions like tobacco, cigarettes, alcohol or drugs?

Yes, Homeopathy can be used as supportive treatment during de-addiction. It may help with concerns such as cravings, irritability, restlessness, anxiety and sleep disturbances during the quitting process. For alcohol or drug dependence, it should be combined with appropriate medical and psychological care.

15. Can Homeopathy help with stress and emotional wellbeing?

Homeopathy considers emotional health as an important part of overall wellness. Treatment may be recommended as part of a broader, individualized approach to supporting emotional balance and stress management.

16. Can Homeopathy be taken by all age groups, including infants, toddlers and the elderly?

Yes. Homeopathy can be used by people of all age groups, from infants and toddlers to adults and the elderly. For children and older adults, medicines should be taken under the guidance of a qualified practitioner.

17. Can homeopathic medicine be taken safely during pregnancy?

Yes, homeopathy is completely safe during pregnancy. Because remedies are prepared from natural substances in gentle, highly diluted doses, they do not pose any toxic risk to you or your developing baby. They provide a safe and effective way to manage common pregnancy discomforts—such as morning sickness, heartburn, fatigue, and lower back pain—without the worry of side effects associated with many conventional drugs.

18. Can homeopathy help with infertility?

Yes, homeopathy can be very supportive in cases of unexplained or functional infertility. Rather than just targeting a single symptom, homeopathy works to correct underlying imbalances in the body. It can help regulate irregular menstrual cycles, manage hormonal disorders like PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome), improve overall reproductive health, and address stress related to conception. By optimizing your body's natural vitality and hormonal balance, it creates a healthier environment for natural conception.

19. Are there any dietary restrictions during Homeopathic treatment?

Yes, certain precautions are recommended. During Homeopathic treatment, it is generally advised to avoid coffee, raw onion, garlic, camphor, strong mint and strong perfumes or aromas, as these may antidote or interfere with the action of the Homeopathic medicine. Dietary changes may also be advised depending on the health condition being treated.

20. Is Homeopathy a replacement for emergency medical care?

No. Homeopathy should never replace emergency medical care. Conditions such as severe breathing difficulty, chest pain, major injuries, heavy bleeding, seizures or loss of consciousness require immediate medical attention.
